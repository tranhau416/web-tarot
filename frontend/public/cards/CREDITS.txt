Rider-Waite-Smith tarot card images
====================================

Artwork:   Pamela Colman Smith (1878-1951), under the direction of
           Arthur Edward Waite (1857-1942)
Publisher: William Rider & Son, London, 1909 ("Pam-A" printing)

Public domain: published 1909 (US), and both creators died more than
70 years ago (life + 70 countries, including the EU and UK, since 2022).

Scans:     Steve-P.org restored scans of a 1909 Pam-A deck
           https://steve-p.org/cards/RWSa.html
           via https://github.com/mixvlad/TarotCards (tarot/rider-waite/720px)
Back:      original "Roses & Lilies" card back (Cover_Rare.jpg)

These are NOT the 1971 U.S. Games Systems recoloured edition.
Resized to 360px WebP by scripts/build_card_images.py.
